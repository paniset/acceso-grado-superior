Ejercicios de desarrollo de Inglés · prueba de acceso a grado superior

6 ejercicios como los de las partes de desarrollo del examen, con solución modelo y casillas para corregirte por criterios.

Cómo usarlo: haz el ejercicio en papel con el reloj puesto. Después abre la solución modelo y marca los criterios que cumple tu respuesta: la nota se calcula en tu navegador y no se guarda ni se envía. Es una estimación: no sustituye a la corrección de un profesor, y los criterios oficiales de tu comunidad pueden repartir los puntos de otra forma.

Ejercicios propios, no copiados de exámenes oficiales. Más práctica: test de Inglés · temario · exámenes oficiales.

Ejercicios

  1. Reading: true or false with justification (40 min)
  2. Complete exam: reading, vocabulary, grammar and writing (60 min)
  3. Writing: informal email (30 min)
  4. Writing: opinion essay (30 min)
  5. Writing: formal email asking for information (30 min)
  6. Grammar: rewrite the sentences (25 min)

1. Reading: true or false with justification

Comprensión lectora · 40 minutos · 10 puntos

When Marta lost her job at a bank in Valencia, she was 41 and had not studied for twenty years. "At first I thought it was too late for me," she says. "Everyone in my course was going to be younger and faster." A friend told her about a two-year vocational course in web development, and she decided to take the entrance exam. She studied every evening after putting her children to bed, and she failed the maths part the first time. The second year she joined a free online group where adults preparing the same exam shared notes and solved problems together. This time she passed with a 7.

Now she works for a small company that designs websites for local shops. She earns a little less than at the bank, but she says she is much happier. "I used to spend the whole day checking numbers that nobody explained to me. Now I can see the results of my work." Her advice for other adults is simple: "Don't study alone, and don't be afraid of failing once. I learnt more from my first exam than from any book."

Read the text and answer the questions in English.

  1. a) Are these sentences true (T) or false (F)? Justify each answer with words from the text. 1. Marta decided to study immediately after losing her job. 2. She passed the entrance exam the first time she took it. 3. The online group did not cost money. 4. Marta earns more money now than in her old job. (4 puntos)
  2. b) Answer in your own words: Why was Marta worried at the beginning of her course? Why does she prefer her new job? (3 puntos)
  3. c) Find words or expressions in the text that mean: 1. not paid for (adj.); 2. gave other people part of something they had (verb, past); 3. recommendation (noun); 4. being unsuccessful in an exam (verb, -ing form). (2 puntos)
  4. d) Rewrite this sentence in the passive: "A small company employs Marta." (1 punto)

La IA puede equivocarse; manda siempre el criterio oficial de tu comunidad.

Ver solución modelo y corregirte

Solución modelo

a) 1. False: "At first I thought it was too late for me" / she only decided after a friend told her about the course. 2. False: "she failed the maths part the first time". 3. True: "she joined a free online group". 4. False: "She earns a little less than at the bank".

b) She was worried because she was 41, she had not studied for many years and she thought the other students would be younger and faster than her. She prefers her new job because she can see the results of her work; at the bank she spent the day checking numbers that nobody explained to her.

c) 1. free; 2. shared; 3. advice; 4. failing.

d) Marta is employed by a small company.

Marca lo que cumple tu respuesta

Nota estimada: 0 sobre 10.

Sin JavaScript, suma tú los puntos de los criterios que cumples y resta los descuentos.

Errores frecuentes

  • Contestar solo «True» o «False» sin copiar la frase que lo demuestra: en la mayoría de exámenes así vale cero.
  • Copiar el párrafo entero en b): se pide «in your own words»; basta con reformular y unir ideas con because, but, so.
  • En la pasiva, cambiar el tiempo verbal («was employed») cuando la activa está en presente.

2. Complete exam: reading, vocabulary, grammar and writing

Comprensión lectora · 60 minutos · 10 puntos

Many cities in Europe are trying to reduce the number of cars in their centres. In Pontevedra, a city in Galicia, the town hall closed most central streets to private traffic in 1999. At first, shop owners were angry because they believed customers would stop coming. However, the opposite happened: more people started walking to the centre, and many families who had left the city came back to live there. Today children walk to school on their own, and the air is much cleaner.

Not everyone agrees, though. Some people who live in nearby villages say it is now harder for them to visit the doctor or do their shopping, because public transport is not frequent enough. Experts say that closing streets is not enough: a city also needs good buses, safe bike lanes and car parks at the entrance to the city.

Read the text and do the exercises. Answer in English.

  1. 1. Answer the questions: a) Why were shop owners angry at first? b) What problem do some people from nearby villages have? (2 puntos)
  2. 2. Find synonyms in the text: a) furious; b) by themselves; c) close / not far; d) secure. (1 punto)
  3. 3. Choose the correct option: a) The streets were closed (by / for / since) the town hall. b) Today the air is (more clean / cleaner / most clean). c) Families (who / which / where) had left came back. d) Experts say closing streets (are / is / be) not enough. (1 punto)
  4. 4. True or false? a) Pontevedra closed its central streets in the 1990s. b) Fewer people walk to the centre now. c) Public transport is very frequent. d) Experts think a city needs more than closed streets. (1 punto)
  5. 5. Grammar: a) Put the verb in the correct tense: "If the buses were more frequent, villagers (visit) the city more often." b) Report: The mayor said: "We will open a new car park next year." (1 punto)
  6. 6. Writing (60-80 words): Would you like to live in a city without cars? Give your opinion and two reasons. (4 puntos)

La IA puede equivocarse; manda siempre el criterio oficial de tu comunidad.

Ver solución modelo y corregirte

Solución modelo

1. a) Because they thought customers would stop coming to their shops. b) It is harder for them to go to the doctor or do their shopping, because public transport is not frequent enough.

2. a) angry; b) on their own; c) nearby; d) safe.

3. a) by; b) cleaner; c) who; d) is.

4. a) True (1999). b) False: more people started walking to the centre. c) False: it is not frequent enough. d) True.

5. a) would visit. b) The mayor said (that) they would open a new car park the following year.

6. I would like to live in a city without cars, for two reasons. Firstly, the air would be cleaner, so people would be healthier and children could play in the street safely. Secondly, it would be quieter, and walking or cycling is a good way to stay fit. However, the city would need good buses for older people and for those who live far away. In my opinion, the advantages are bigger than the problems. (76 words)

Nota estimada: 0 sobre 10.

Errores frecuentes

  • En el estilo indirecto, dejar «will» y «next year»: cambian a «would» y «the following year».
  • «More clean»: los adjetivos de una sílaba forman el comparativo con -er.
  • Escribir 120 palabras en la redacción cuando se piden 60-80: el exceso suele penalizar y quita tiempo al resto.

3. Writing: informal email

Redacción: email y opinion essay · 30 minutos · 10 puntos

Your English friend Sam is coming to visit your town next month for a weekend. Write an email to Sam (100-120 words). Include: where you will take Sam and why; a typical food Sam must try; what clothes Sam should bring; a question about Sam's plans.

  1. Write the email (100-120 words) including the four points. (10 puntos)

La IA puede equivocarse; manda siempre el criterio oficial de tu comunidad.

Ver solución modelo y corregirte

Solución modelo

Hi Sam,

I'm so happy you're coming next month! I've already made some plans. On Saturday morning I'll take you to the old town, because the streets and the castle are beautiful. In the afternoon we can go to the beach if the weather is good.

You must try paella. My grandmother makes the best one in town, and she has invited us for lunch on Sunday!

It's usually warm during the day, but it gets cold at night, so bring a jacket and comfortable shoes for walking.

By the way, what time does your train arrive? I can pick you up at the station.

See you soon,

Laura

(109 words)

Nota estimada: 0 sobre 10.

Errores frecuentes

  • Olvidar uno de los cuatro puntos: es la forma más rápida de perder nota.
  • Mezclar registros: «Dear Sir» o «Yours faithfully» en un email a un amigo.
  • Preguntas sin auxiliar: «What time your train arrives?» en vez de «What time does your train arrive?».

4. Writing: opinion essay

Redacción: email y opinion essay · 30 minutos · 10 puntos

"Young people spend too much time on their mobile phones." Do you agree? Write an opinion essay (100-120 words) with an introduction, two or three arguments and a conclusion.

  1. Write the opinion essay (100-120 words). (10 puntos)

La IA puede equivocarse; manda siempre el criterio oficial de tu comunidad.

Ver solución modelo y corregirte

Solución modelo

Nowadays almost every teenager has a smartphone, and many adults think they use it too much. In my opinion, this is partly true.

On the one hand, a lot of young people check their phones constantly, even during meals or at night, and this can affect their sleep and their marks at school. Moreover, spending hours on social media may make them feel worse about themselves.

On the other hand, phones are also useful tools: students use them to look up information, learn languages or keep in touch with friends who live far away.

To sum up, I believe the problem is not the phone itself but how we use it. Setting limits is the best solution.

(117 words)

Errores frecuentes

  • Escribir una lista de ideas sin explicarlas: cada argumento necesita un porqué o un ejemplo.
  • «People is» o «the young people spends»: people es plural.
  • Traducir literalmente del español («I am agree»): se dice «I agree».

5. Writing: formal email asking for information

Redacción: email y opinion essay · 30 minutos · 10 puntos

You have seen an advertisement for a free evening course in computer skills at a local adult education centre. Write a formal email to the centre (100-120 words). Say who you are and why you are interested; ask about the timetable and the start date; ask what level is required; ask how to register.

  1. Write the formal email (100-120 words) including all the points. (10 puntos)

La IA puede equivocarse; manda siempre el criterio oficial de tu comunidad.

Ver solución modelo y corregirte

Solución modelo

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to ask for information about the evening course in computer skills which I saw advertised on your website.

I am 35 years old and I work in a supermarket. I would like to improve my computer skills because I am planning to take the entrance exam for a vocational course next year.

Could you tell me what days the classes take place and when the course starts? I would also like to know whether students need any previous knowledge. Finally, could you explain how I can register?

I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,

Javier Ruiz

(105 words)

Nota estimada: 0 sobre 10.

Errores frecuentes

  • Usar «Yours sincerely» cuando no sabes el nombre del destinatario: con «Dear Sir or Madam» va «Yours faithfully».
  • Preguntas indirectas con el orden de una pregunta directa: «Could you tell me when does the course start?» → «when the course starts».
  • Contracciones («I'm», «don't») en un email formal.

6. Grammar: rewrite the sentences

La voz pasiva · 25 minutos · 10 puntos

Rewrite the sentences so that they mean the same as the first one. Use the word or the beginning given.

  1. 1. They built this bridge in 1890. → This bridge ... (1 punto)
  2. 2. Somebody is repairing my car. → My car ... (1 punto)
  3. 3. "I am tired," Ana said. → Ana said ... (1 punto)
  4. 4. "Have you finished your homework?" my mother asked me. → My mother asked me ... (1 punto)
  5. 5. I don't have a car, so I can't drive to work. → If I ... (1 punto)
  6. 6. You will fail if you don't study. (UNLESS) → You will fail ... (1 punto)
  7. 7. I met a woman. She speaks five languages. (WHO) → I met ... (1 punto)
  8. 8. This is the town. I was born there. (WHERE) → This is ... (1 punto)
  9. 9. It isn't necessary to bring food. (HAVE) → You ... (1 punto)
  10. 10. I started working here three years ago. (FOR) → I have ... (1 punto)

La IA puede equivocarse; manda siempre el criterio oficial de tu comunidad.

Ver solución modelo y corregirte

Solución modelo

1. This bridge was built in 1890.

2. My car is being repaired.

3. Ana said (that) she was tired.

4. My mother asked me if/whether I had finished my homework.

5. If I had a car, I could drive to work.

6. You will fail unless you study.

7. I met a woman who speaks five languages.

8. This is the town where I was born.

9. You don't have to bring food.

10. I have worked / have been working here for three years.

Marca lo que cumple tu respuesta

Nota estimada: 0 sobre 10.

Sin JavaScript, suma tú los puntos de los criterios que cumples y resta los descuentos.

Errores frecuentes

  • «Unless you don't study»: unless ya es negativo (= if not).
  • Confundir «mustn't» (prohibido) con «don't have to» (no hace falta).
  • En la pregunta indirecta, mantener el orden de pregunta: «asked me had I finished» → «asked me if I had finished».
